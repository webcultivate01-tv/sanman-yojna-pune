🚑 24/7 Ambulance Service

A death rarely waits for a convenient hour. Our hearse and ambulance service runs round the clock — one phone call and a trained team is dispatched to bring your loved one home, or from home to the crematorium, with care and respect at every step.

Door-to-crematorium transport anywhere within Pune & Pimpri-Chinchwad city limits.

Trained attendants who handle the shifting and the paperwork, including the death certificate.

🕊️ Complete Funeral Materials

Nobody should be sent to a market at a time like this. Every item needed for the last rites arrives with our team — the bier, the earthen pot, the garlands, the samagri — measured, arranged and ready.

  • Earthen pot (Matke)

  • Flowers & garlands

  • Kumkum, haldi, akshata
  • Ghee, til, incense, attar

Service 03

🌊 Ash Immersion (Asthi Visarjan)

The collection of ashes and their immersion is one of the hardest days for a family. We arrange the entire journey — from the crematorium to the sacred water and back — so that all you have to do is be present.

  1. 1

    Collection of ashes

    Handled respectfully at the crematorium, with the urn and cloth provided.

  2. 2

    Vehicle & priest arranged

    Transport for up to five family members, along with a purohit to lead the rites.

  3. 3

    Immersion with full ritual

    Performed at the sacred site your family chooses, with all samagri included.

🛕 Religious Rituals by Experienced Priests

Our panel of experienced purohits, maulvis and fathers has guided thousands of families through the last rites. Whatever your faith and however your family has always done it, the ritual is performed properly — nothing skipped, nothing improvised.

Antyasanskar at the crematorium, cemetery or the place your tradition prescribes.

Dashakriya & 13th-day rites with the priest, samagri and meal arrangements taken care of.

Priest honorarium included — no separate negotiation, no surprise cost on the day.

🤝 Family Counseling & Coordination

The arrangements end in a day. The grief does not.

A single coordinator stays with your family from the first call through the tenth day — reminding you what comes next, making the calls you cannot bring yourself to make, and simply sitting with you when that is what is needed. Grief counselling support is part of the plan, not an add-on.
