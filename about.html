About Sanman Yojana

Our Purpose & Values

For 25 years we have been guided by one vision — to stand beside families in Pune when they need it most, and to make a dignified farewell something no household has to worry about.

Where we are headed

Our Vision

To be the most trusted funeral service in Maharashtra — the organisation that sets the standard for compassion, transparency and cultural sensitivity. We are working towards a Pune where no family, of any faith or income, faces a loss alone or unprepared.

  • ✓A dignified farewell within reach of every household budget
  • ✓Equal respect for every faith, community and tradition we serve
  • ✓Support that continues well past the funeral day itself
Leadership

Founder's Profile

Sanman Yojana is run under Shahid Bhagat Singh Pratishthan, Pune — a registered public trust working in community welfare and last-rites assistance.

Message from the Founder

“No family should have to bargain over a farewell. That belief is the whole reason this organisation exists.”

Vijay More established Sanman Yojana in 2004 with a simple conviction — that compassion and transparency should be the foundation of funeral services. What began as an effort to help a handful of neighbouring families has grown into a trusted institution serving more than 4,200 households across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

With deep roots in community service and a lifelong commitment to cultural respect, he has built the organisation on three principles that have never changed: dignity for the departed, honesty with the family, and help that arrives before it is asked for. Under his leadership Sanman Yojana has become a name families across the district pass on to one another.
